# Card dataset notes

The 78 entries in `data/cards.csv` are concise original learning summaries inspired by the Rider-Waite-Smith card structure. They are not verbatim transcriptions from Waite or another author. The file separates:

- upright and reversed keywords;
- technical explanation and simple explanation;
- suggested image observations and reflection prompts;
- the optional Golden Dawn / Book T correspondence and its system label.

The Major Arcana use the common Golden Dawn attributions for the element, planet or zodiac sign. The numbered Minor Arcana Two–Ten use the 36 Golden Dawn decans. Aces are assigned to the four suit elements. Court cards use the elemental formula King=Fire, Queen=Water, Knight=Air and Page=Earth of the suit; no one-sign shortcut is imposed.

This layer is designed for comparative symbolic study. It should not be represented as an ancient universal tarot rule or scientific evidence.
